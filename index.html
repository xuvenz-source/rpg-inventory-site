Built for character sheets

A separate inventory for every sheet.

Each Google Sheets document using RPG Inventory stores its inventory information within that document, rather than in a shared website database. When each player has their own character spreadsheet, their items remain in that spreadsheet.

How do I open it?

In a character spreadsheet configured with RPG Inventory, open the Inventory menu and choose Open inventory. The sidebar loads and saves items to that sheet. Availability and installation instructions depend on how your GM or the publisher has distributed the script.

If multiple people have access to the same spreadsheet, those people may also be able to access its inventory. Hidden storage tabs are not a security or permission boundary.

Transparency

Know where your items go.

Inventory data and optional item images are stored in hidden sheets inside your Google spreadsheet. This website does not ask you to sign in or upload inventory data.
