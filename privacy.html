RPG Inventory

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026

This policy explains how RPG Inventory, a Google Apps Script inventory sidebar for Google Sheets, accesses, uses, stores and shares information when you use the application.

1. Information the application accesses

When you use the inventory sidebar, it accesses the Google Sheets spreadsheet in which it was opened. The information processed can include the spreadsheet ID and name, the inventory grid dimensions and limits, item names and abbreviations, descriptions, positions and sizes, quantities, weights, colours, and any images you choose to attach to items.

The application requires Google authorization to access the spreadsheet features needed to operate. The permissions shown by Google during authorization determine what your installed version is authorized to access.

2. Why the information is used

This information is used to display your inventory, create and update items, move or rotate blocks on the grid, calculate carrying weight, display item images, and save changes to the correct Google spreadsheet. The application does not need your inventory for advertising.

3. Storage

In the current spreadsheet-bound version of RPG Inventory, item data is written to a hidden worksheet named _InventoryData. Compressed item images are written to a separate hidden worksheet named _InventoryImages, both inside the same Google Sheets file. The application does not use a separate developer-operated inventory database. Data remains in the spreadsheet until it is changed or deleted in that document or the spreadsheet itself is deleted, subject to Google Drive retention and recovery features.

4. Sharing and access

RPG Inventory does not sell or use your Google Sheets inventory information for advertising. The current version of the script does not transmit inventory contents to an independent external server. Google processes spreadsheet data as part of providing Google Sheets and Apps Script. Other people with access to a shared spreadsheet may also be able to see or change its inventory, depending on their Google Sheets permissions. Hidden tabs are not private or encrypted. If you want separate inventories for separate players, use separate spreadsheet files and restrict access to each file.

5. Uploaded images

If you choose to attach an image to an item, the browser reduces its dimensions and saves compressed image data in the spreadsheet's hidden image worksheet. The image is displayed in the sidebar when that inventory is loaded. Do not upload sensitive images that you would not want other spreadsheet collaborators to access.

6. How to delete your data

You can delete items through the inventory interface, including their stored image entries. You can also delete the spreadsheet or remove the inventory storage worksheets if you no longer need the inventory, though manually altering storage sheets may break the application. Spreadsheet owners can manage access through Google Drive sharing controls. Copies of a spreadsheet contain their own copy of the inventory data until separately removed.

7. This website and third-party services

This informational website does not ask you to sign in and does not itself collect item contents, credentials or payment data. It does not contain third-party advertising or intentionally installed analytics trackers. The website host may process technical requests, such as IP addresses, to deliver the site and maintain security. Clicking a link to Discord, GitHub or Google takes you to a separate service governed by its own privacy practices.

8. Changes to this policy

If the application's data handling changes, this page should be updated before the changed behaviour is made available. The effective date above identifies the version of the policy displayed here.

9. Contact

For questions or requests about RPG Inventory and this policy, contact the developer via the RPG Inventory support community on Discord. You may also review the developer's GitHub profile. For concerns involving a shared character spreadsheet, contact the owner of that Google Sheet as well.

Scope of this policy: This policy describes the current standalone spreadsheet-bound inventory code. Any future campaign sharing, item transfer, cloud storage or administrator tools must be reviewed and disclosed here before they are enabled in a published application.
